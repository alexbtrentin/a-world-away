Install on iPhone

  1. Open this game’s HTTPS link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an up arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App enabled when shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open A World Away from the Home Screen and turn your phone sideways.

After the game loads over HTTPS, its files will be cached for offline play. Install before beginning so progress is saved in the Home Screen app.
